# Market notes: lofi focus and desktop companion games on Steam

Research done November 2026. Sales figures from third parties are estimates.

## What the category looks like

- **Spirit City: Lofi Sessions** (Mooncube Games, April 2024) is the reference title: a gamified focus tool with lofi music, room and avatar customisation, a pomodoro timer, to-do list, habit tracker and collectable companions. A three-person team sold more than 55,000 copies in its first week. Base price US$11.99, now with five DLC packs and many bundles.
- **Lofi Haven** (February 2026, US$7.99) combines a focus tool with full room decoration: the closest match to Midnighty Room.
- **Virtual Cottage** and **Chill Corner** are free, minimal solo focus companions. Virtual Cottage 2 had about 58,000 wishlists in early 2026.
- Steam added an official **Desktop Companion** tag in 2026, so small always-on-screen apps are now directly searchable.
- New entries keep arriving (FocusScape, Lo-Fi with Ruka, Lofi Aquarium, Study With Me), so the niche is active and getting crowded.

## What the successful ones share

- A cute, cozy look that matches "lofi study" YouTube thumbnails. Art and music carry the purchase decision.
- Real productivity tools (timer, tasks, stats) wrapped in light progression: focusing unlocks cosmetics.
- A playable demo. Spirit City's wishlists grew slowly for seven months until the demo.
- Localisation. Spirit City's developer reported over 43% of sales from Asian countries.
- Coverage from cozy-game content creators rather than big streamers.
- DLC cosmetic packs and bundles with other cozy games after launch.

## What this means for Midnighty Room

| Decision | Reasoning |
|---|---|
| Price US$5.99 to US$7.99 | Below Spirit City, level with Lofi Haven, above the free tools. |
| Lean on decoration and the mini window | Full drag-and-drop decorating and a desktop-companion mode are the differentiators already built. |
| Replace the generated art and music before launch | The current assets prove the game works; they will not compete on a store page. |
| Ship a demo early | Steam Next Fest plus a demo is the proven wishlist driver in this niche. |
| Localise early | Chinese, Japanese and Korean first. |
| Plan cosmetic DLC | Room themes and item packs fit the existing catalogue system. |

Honest expectation: most indie releases earn little. This niche has demonstrated demand, but results depend on art, music and marketing far more than on code.
